Leadership principles

How I run programs

The operating beliefs that shape how I lead teams and deliver at scale.

🎯

Clarity beats control

Crisp goals, scope, and ownership outperform heavy process. I optimize for shared understanding, not status theater.

🔍

Surface risk early

Bad news doesn’t age well. I make dependencies and risks visible while there’s still time to act on them.

🤝

Earn trust, then move fast

Credibility with engineers and executives is the currency that lets a program move at speed.

📊

Decide with data

Metrics frame the conversation. I instrument outcomes so decisions are grounded, not anecdotal.

🧭

Bias for outcomes

Shipping value beats shipping activity. Every ritual must earn its place by moving the result.

🌱

Grow the team

Programs end; people stay. I leave teams more capable, aligned, and confident than I found them.
